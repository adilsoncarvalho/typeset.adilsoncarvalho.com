1 · Tie

A non-breaking join between a figure and its unit, a section mark and its number — so a line break can never fall between them, wherever the paragraph happens to wrap.

Keep a figure with its unit: 11 pt, §2.1, Fig. 1 — none of them ever break across a line.

2 · No hyphenation

Stops the dictionary from breaking one specific run — a name, a trademark — while everything else in a justified paragraph keeps hyphenating as usual. Disabling hyphenation for a whole justified paragraph is the mistake the justification section shows; this narrows the override to the one word that must never take a hyphen.

The village of Llanfairpwllgwyngyllgogerychwyrndrobwllllantysiliogogogoch sits on Anglesey, and this justified paragraph is left to hyphenate its name wherever the dictionary allows, the same as any other word here.
