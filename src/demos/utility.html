3 · Keep together

Never lets a page break fall inside this element — a short table, a signature block, a callout. Where the room left on the page is not enough for the whole of it, all of it moves to the next page rather than half of it staying behind.

4 · No hyphenation

Stops the dictionary from breaking one specific run — a name, a trademark — while everything else in a justified paragraph keeps hyphenating as usual. Disabling hyphenation for a whole justified paragraph is the mistake the justification section shows; this narrows the override to the one word that must never take a hyphen.

The village of Llanfairpwllgwyngyllgogerychwyrndrobwllllantysiliogogogoch sits on Anglesey, and this justified paragraph is left to hyphenate its name wherever the dictionary allows, the same as any other word here.

The village of Llanfairpwllgwyngyllgogerychwyrndrobwllllantysiliogogogoch sits on Anglesey, and this justified paragraph must never hyphenate that one name, however freely it hyphenates every other word here.

5 · Tie

A non-breaking join between a figure and its unit, a section mark and its number — so a line break can never fall between them, wherever the paragraph happens to wrap.

Keep a figure with its unit: 11 pt, §2.1, Fig. 1 — none of them ever break across a line.

6 · Print only / screen only

A pair: one class hides its element on screen, the other hides it on paper. Exactly one of the two sentences below is present in any medium, and the one you can see here is the screen's.

Every source is linked where it is cited — follow any of them for the full reference.

Every source is listed in full on the last page, because a page has no links to follow.

Printed, the sentence above disappears and this one takes its place: Every source is listed in full on the last page, because a page has no links to follow. Never both, and never neither.

7 · Exact colour

Print dialogs save ink by leaving out background graphics. A zebra table whose bands are left out is not a plainer table — it is a table whose rows have lost the thing that told them apart. This forces every background and rule in the document through to the paper instead.
