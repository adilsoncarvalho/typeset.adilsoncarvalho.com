1 · Level-2 number

Generated by a counter, never typed — it cannot drift out of sync when a section moves, and it disappears cleanly wherever typeset--numbered is not applied. Each pane here is its own document, so each starts counting again from one; the Typst tab sets the same sections as one continuous document, and numbers them accordingly.

Scope

The opening section.

Method

A second top-level section — the counter advanced on its own, with nobody typing a number.

2 · Level-3 number

The level-2 counter names the section a subsection belongs to; the level-3 counter resets the moment a new level-2 section begins.

Method

A second top-level section — the counter advanced on its own, with nobody typing a number.

Instrumentation

A subsection, numbered from the section that contains it.

Results

Baseline

The level-3 counter reset when the new level-2 section began.
