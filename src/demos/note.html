1 · Reference mark

Raised, without brackets, and without opening up the line's own leading — the mark must not stretch the line it sits in.

Bringhurst puts the ideal measure between 45 and 75 characters, a range narrow enough to be useful and wide enough to survive a change of typeface.

2 · Footnote

The note text lives in the document, marked .ts-note-footnote. Where the engine implements the CSS footnote model, that promotes it to the foot of the page carrying the mark; no browser does, so what a screen shows is the fallback below — the same numbered block either way.

The lower bound matters more in a two-column layout, where the column is the measure and there is no slack to give away.

3 · Endnotes block

No heading. The rule above the list and the numbered items already read as a notes area, and a label over two lines of small print is heavier than the thing it labels.

Bringhurst puts the ideal measure between 45 and 75 characters, a range narrow enough to be useful and wide enough to survive a change of typeface. The lower bound matters more in a two-column layout.
