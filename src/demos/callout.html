1 · Callout

Serif, on a wash, with a rule down the left edge — set apart from the argument without shouting. An aside the reader may skip without losing the thread.

Point sizes assume the document is printed at 100%. “Fit to page” scaling in a print dialog silently invalidates every measurement in this stylesheet.

2 · Callout title

Sans, small, uppercase, tracked wide, muted — a label for the callout below it, not a heading competing with the document's own.

Note

Fonts must be embedded in the exported PDF, or the recipient's own installed fonts silently take over.

3 · Callout — warning

The same shape, in the accent colour — one variant only, alongside the neutral one, because a document with five callout colours has none.

Before you send it

Check that fonts are embedded in the PDF. A document that falls back to Times on the recipient's machine has lost every decision above.
