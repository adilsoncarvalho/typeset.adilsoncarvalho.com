Heading

Sans, bold, 18pt — the same weight and register as any other top-level heading, so a bibliography reads as a section of the document rather than an appendix bolted on.

References

The entries follow immediately, each hanging-indented so the surname stays the leftmost thing on the line.

Entry

Hanging indent, always. The surname is what the eye is scanning for, so it must be the leftmost token — an indented first line puts it where the eye is not looking.

  2. Hochuli, Jost. Detail in Typography. Hyphen Press, 2008.
  3. Ruder, Emil. Typographie: A Manual of Design. Niggli, 1967. Still the clearest statement of the case for the grid, and long enough ago to be read without argument.
  4. Tschichold, Jan. The Form of the Book. Hartley & Marks, 1991.
