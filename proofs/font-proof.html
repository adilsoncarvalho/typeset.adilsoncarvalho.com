Printing this proof. Six candidates, one per page. Judge on paper, not on screen.
  • Print at 100%. Turn off “fit to page” / “scale to fit” — it invalidates every point size here.
  • Use the paper and the printer you will actually use. A laser and an inkjet fail light hairlines differently.
  • Read the small-sizes block first. That is where a display face gives itself away; the 11pt sample flatters everything.
  • Then read the two-column block at arm's length. If you can read it without leaning in, it works at 9.5pt.

Candidate 1 of 6

EB Garamond

Text face. The incumbent. A Garamond revival drawn for reading sizes — but its own note in the spec says no smaller than 11pt.

Single column · 11pt / 1.45 · 33em measure

Ask anyone to improve a document and they will change the font. It is the most visible lever and the least effective one. The reader who abandoned your report on page three did not abandon it because the type was thin; they abandoned it because each line ran a hundred and ten characters wide and their eye could not find the start of the next.

Two column · 9.5pt / 1.4 · 86mm columns · justified, hyphenated

This is not a matter of taste. The return sweep — the movement from the end of one line to the beginning of the next — is a motor task, and like every motor task it has an error rate that rises with distance. Past about seventy-five characters the rate becomes high enough that a reader begins re-reading lines without noticing. What they notice is fatigue, and fatigue gets attributed to the content rather than to its setting. The conventional figure is forty-five to seventy-five characters for a single column, with sixty-six given as the ideal, and the width of that range is the interesting part.

The small sizes — where a display face fails first

9.5pt — the two-column body size. Judge the stems and the hairlines here, not on the page above.

8.5pt — table and endnote size. Are the thin strokes continuous, or are they breaking up?

8pt — caption and running-head size. This is the floor. If it looks grey rather than black, the face is too light for print at this size.
